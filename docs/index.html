Whole web pages

Starts quietly

The Atlas Helper sits in the tray. No console and no window open when it starts. Open it from the tray icon when you need it.

Install in a few minutes

Get the extension for your browser, then the Atlas Helper if you want offline translation, PDFs on your computer, and the voices.

1. The browser extension

  • Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Download the package for your browser from the releases page.
  • Pin the toolbar button, then open the translator from the menu.

2. The Atlas Helper

  • Windows, macOS and Linux. It runs in the tray, with no console window.
  • Offline translation, PDF translation and the voices come from the helper's Offline tab.
